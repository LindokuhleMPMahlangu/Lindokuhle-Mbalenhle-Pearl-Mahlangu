01 — A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Different field.
Same curious mind.

I used to solve problems in chemical processes. Now I’m learning to solve them with software, cloud architecture, and a lot of curiosity.

My journey from process engineering into tech taught me that the best solutions start with understanding people and the systems around them. Today, I’m studying software engineering at WeThinkCode_ while exploring Java, AWS serverless, and Python automation.
